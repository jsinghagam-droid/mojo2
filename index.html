My Isha,

I don't think words will ever be enough. But I'll keep trying, because you deserve every attempt — every imperfect sentence, every honest feeling I carry for you. You came into my life and quietly rearranged everything. Not loudly, not all at once. Just slowly, the way dawn arrives — until suddenly the whole world looked different and I couldn't remember what it felt like before you.

I love you in ways I'm still discovering. I love the version of you that laughs too loudly at your own jokes. I love the version of you that's soft and quiet at 2am. And I love the version of you that pushes through even when everything feels impossible — I see that version more than you think. Your strength isn't loud. It's the kind that bends without breaking. The kind that keeps going even when no one is watching.

I am so profoundly proud of you. Not for what you've achieved — though that is remarkable — but for who you are beneath all of it. For the tenderness you carry. For the way you love people without being asked. For the way you fight for yourself even on the days you forget you're worth fighting for. I haven't forgotten, Isha. I never will.

I count myself among the luckiest people alive, simply because I get to know you. Because I've seen you at your most ordinary and your most magnificent, and both have left me breathless. You are not background. You are not a side character. You are the whole story — and every version of it is beautiful.

Thank you for trusting me. Thank you for your realness, your warmth, your patience, your softness. Thank you for existing in this exact shape, at this exact time, in my exact life. I don't take a single moment of you for granted.

Yours, completely —
Agam
